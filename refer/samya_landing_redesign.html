01 / The problem

A good match asks for more than a shade number.

Finding foundation is rarely as simple as choosing between light and dark. Skin tone, undertone, testing conditions and how a formula wears can all shape the experience.

01

Limited choice.

Finding a shade that accounts for your skin tone and undertone can be difficult.

02

Limited testing.

Online swatches and store lighting don't always tell you how a shade looks on your own skin.

03

Uncertain wear.

A shade that looks right at first may change in appearance as the formula settles and wears.

02 / Our approach

Your skin. Your shade. Your experience.

We're developing a growing shade library and a guided discovery process. Real-world feedback will help inform which shades we refine and make available.
